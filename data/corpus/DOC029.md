# Atal Pension Yojana (APY)

Atal Pension Yojana is a pension scheme for workers in the unorganised sector, launched on 9 May 2015 and administered by the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority. Any citizen between the ages of 18 and 40 years who has a savings bank account can join. The scheme guarantees a fixed minimum monthly pension of Rs 1,000, Rs 2,000, Rs 3,000, Rs 4,000 or Rs 5,000 after the subscriber reaches the age of 60, depending on the contribution. The contribution depends on the age of joining and the pension chosen, so a younger entrant pays a smaller monthly amount, and it is auto-debited from the savings account. After the death of the subscriber the spouse receives the same pension, and after the death of both the nominee receives the accumulated pension corpus. For subscribers who joined before 31 March 2016 and were not income-tax payers, the Government paid a co-contribution of 50 percent of the contribution or Rs 1,000 per year, whichever was lower, for five years. From 1 October 2022 income-tax payers are not eligible to join the scheme.
