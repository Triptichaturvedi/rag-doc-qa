# Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY)

Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana is the National Mission for Financial Inclusion, launched on 28 August 2014 to ensure access to financial services for every household. Under the scheme, people can open a basic savings bank account with zero balance in any bank branch or through a Business Correspondent. Account holders receive a RuPay debit card with an inbuilt accident insurance cover, which is Rs 2 lakh for accounts opened after 28 August 2018 and Rs 1 lakh for accounts opened earlier. An overdraft facility of up to Rs 10,000 is available to eligible account holders. Accounts are linked with Aadhaar and mobile numbers so that Direct Benefit Transfer of government subsidies can reach beneficiaries directly, which reduces leakage. The scheme also gives access to insurance, pension and credit products. More than 50 crore accounts have been opened since launch. The Ministry of Finance, Department of Financial Services, runs the mission, and a Know Your Customer document such as Aadhaar is generally sufficient to open an account.
