# Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY)

Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana was launched on 1 May 2016 from Ballia in Uttar Pradesh by the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas to provide clean cooking fuel to women from poor households and reduce the use of firewood and cow dung. The scheme provides a deposit-free LPG connection in the name of an adult woman from a Below Poverty Line household, with financial support of Rs 1,600 per connection to cover the cost of the security deposit, pressure regulator and other items. The initial target of five crore connections was raised to eight crore and was achieved in September 2019. Ujjwala 2.0 was launched in August 2021 with a target of one crore additional connections and relaxed documentation for migrant families, who can give a self-declaration as proof of address and family composition. Oil marketing companies release the connections through LPG distributors. A targeted subsidy of Rs 300 per 14.2 kg cylinder for up to twelve refills a year has been given to beneficiaries in recent years, but the amount is reviewed periodically and should be checked.
