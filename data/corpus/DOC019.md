# Panchayati Raj and Municipalities (73rd and 74th Amendments)

The 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act of 1992, which came into force on 24 April 1993, added Part IX to the Constitution on Panchayats (Articles 243 to 243O) and the Eleventh Schedule listing 29 subjects. It provides for a three-tier system of village, intermediate and district panchayats, although States with a population below twenty lakh may skip the intermediate level. The Gram Sabha, consisting of all registered voters in a village, is the foundation of the system. Panchayat elections are held every five years and are conducted by a State Election Commission. Not less than one-third of the seats, including the posts of chairpersons, are reserved for women, and seats are reserved for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes in proportion to their population. A State Finance Commission reviews the finances of panchayats every five years. The 74th Amendment Act of 1992 added Part IXA on Municipalities and the Twelfth Schedule listing 18 subjects, and it came into force on 1 June 1993. National Panchayati Raj Day is observed on 24 April.
