# Swachh Bharat Mission (Gramin and Urban)

The Swachh Bharat Mission was launched on 2 October 2014, the 145th birth anniversary of Mahatma Gandhi, with the aim of achieving a clean India by 2 October 2019. The rural component, Swachh Bharat Mission Gramin, focused on building household toilets and ending open defecation, and on 2 October 2019 the Government declared all villages open defecation free. Phase II of the rural mission, running from 2020-21 to 2024-25, concentrated on sustaining open defecation free status and on solid and liquid waste management, a stage called ODF Plus. The urban component was managed by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs, and Swachh Bharat Mission Urban 2.0 was launched on 1 October 2021 for five years with the vision of garbage-free cities, safe sanitation and used-water management in cities. Citizens are encouraged to give feedback through the Swachhata app and the annual Swachh Survekshan ranks cities on cleanliness. Verified outcomes such as toilet use and long-term sustainability have been studied by independent researchers and are debated.
