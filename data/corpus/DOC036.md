# NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0

The NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0, published in February 2024, is a voluntary framework that helps organisations of any size and sector understand, assess, prioritise and communicate cybersecurity risks. Its Core is organised into six functions: Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond and Recover. Govern is the new function added in version 2.0, and it covers the organisation's cybersecurity strategy, expectations, policy and oversight, while version 1.1 had only five functions. Identify means understanding assets, suppliers and risks; Protect covers safeguards such as access control, training and data security; Detect covers continuous monitoring to find anomalies and attacks; Respond covers incident management, analysis and communication; and Recover covers restoring assets and operations after an incident. Each function is divided into categories and subcategories that describe outcomes rather than specific tools. Organisational Profiles describe the current and target cybersecurity posture so that gaps can be planned. Implementation Tiers, from Tier 1 Partial to Tier 4 Adaptive, describe how rigorous an organisation's risk governance and management practices are.
