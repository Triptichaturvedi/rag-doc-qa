# Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) - Urban and Gramin

Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana aims to provide pucca houses with basic amenities to eligible families under the Housing for All mission. PMAY-Urban was launched on 25 June 2015 and had four verticals: In-situ Slum Redevelopment, Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme, Affordable Housing in Partnership and Beneficiary-led Construction. In September 2024 the Government approved PMAY-Urban 2.0 to support one crore urban families over five years. PMAY-Gramin was launched on 20 November 2016 and gives financial assistance for constructing a house with a minimum area of 25 square metres. The unit assistance is Rs 1.20 lakh in plain areas and Rs 1.30 lakh in hilly states, difficult areas and Integrated Action Plan districts. Beneficiaries are also entitled to convergence support for a toilet under the Swachh Bharat Mission. In 2024 the Cabinet approved assistance for two crore additional rural houses over the period 2024-25 to 2028-29. Houses are generally registered in the name of the woman or jointly with the husband.
