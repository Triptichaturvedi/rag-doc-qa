# Right to Freedom (Articles 19-22)

The Right to Freedom is contained in Articles 19 to 22. Article 19(1) guarantees six freedoms to citizens: freedom of speech and expression, freedom to assemble peaceably and without arms, freedom to form associations or unions or cooperative societies, freedom to move freely throughout India, freedom to reside and settle in any part of India, and freedom to practise any profession or carry on any occupation, trade or business. These freedoms are subject to reasonable restrictions. Article 20 gives protection in respect of conviction for offences: no ex post facto criminal law, no double jeopardy and no compulsion to be a witness against oneself. Article 21 provides that no person shall be deprived of life or personal liberty except according to procedure established by law, and Article 21A, inserted by the 86th Amendment in 2002, makes free and compulsory education a right for children aged six to fourteen. Article 22 protects persons who are arrested or detained, including the right to be informed of the grounds of arrest, to consult a lawyer and to be produced before a magistrate within 24 hours.
