# Constitutional Bodies and the Official Language (Articles 148, 280, 315, 324, 343)

Article 324 vests the superintendence, direction and control of elections to Parliament, State Legislatures and the offices of the President and Vice-President in the Election Commission of India, and the Chief Election Commissioner can be removed only in the same manner and on the same grounds as a judge of the Supreme Court. Article 326 provides for elections on the basis of adult suffrage. Article 148 establishes the Comptroller and Auditor-General of India, who audits the accounts of the Union and the States and is appointed by the President for a term of six years or until the age of 65, whichever is earlier. Article 315 provides for the Union Public Service Commission and Article 280 for a Finance Commission, which is constituted every five years to recommend the distribution of tax revenues between the Centre and the States. Under Article 343 the official language of the Union is Hindi in the Devanagari script, and the Official Languages Act of 1963 allows the continued use of English. The Eighth Schedule lists 22 languages.
