# National Food Security Act, 2013 (NFSA)

The National Food Security Act, 2013 gives legal entitlement to subsidised foodgrains to up to 75 percent of the rural population and 50 percent of the urban population through the Targeted Public Distribution System. Priority households are entitled to 5 kg of foodgrains per person per month, while Antyodaya Anna Yojana households, the poorest of the poor, receive 35 kg per household per month. The issue prices were fixed at Rs 3 per kg for rice, Rs 2 per kg for wheat and Rs 1 per kg for coarse grains. The Act also provides maternity benefits of at least Rs 6,000 to pregnant and lactating women and nutritious meals to children up to fourteen years of age through the Integrated Child Development Services and the Mid-Day Meal scheme. The eldest woman in the household, aged 18 or above, is treated as the head of the household for issuing ration cards. From 1 January 2023 the foodgrains under the Act have been supplied free of cost, and in 2023 the Cabinet extended the free supply for five years from 1 January 2024.
