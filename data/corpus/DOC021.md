# PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)

PM-KISAN is a Central Sector scheme of the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare that gives income support to landholding farmer families. Eligible families receive Rs 6,000 per year in three equal instalments of Rs 2,000 each, paid every four months through Direct Benefit Transfer into Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts. The scheme was launched in February 2019 and implemented with effect from December 2018, and the Union Budget 2026-27 kept the allocation at over Rs 60,000 crore. It is fully funded by the Central Government. Initially limited to small and marginal farmers, it was extended in 2019 to all landholding farmer families. Higher-income categories are excluded, including institutional landholders, current or former holders of constitutional posts, serving or retired government officers other than multi-tasking and Group D staff, income-tax payers and professionals such as doctors, engineers and lawyers. Beneficiaries must complete e-KYC and keep their Aadhaar and bank details linked. Reports in 2026 indicate that the scheme has been extended up to 2030-31; farmers should confirm current details on the official portal.
