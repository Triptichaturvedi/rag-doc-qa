# MGNREGA and its Successor VB-G RAM G (Rural Employment Guarantee)

The Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act, 2005 (MGNREGA) gave every rural household a legal right to 100 days of unskilled manual wage employment in a financial year. A worker who applied for work had to be given employment within fifteen days, failing which an unemployment allowance was payable. At least one-third of the beneficiaries had to be women, and work was to be provided within five kilometres of the village where possible. Gram Panchayats planned the works. From 1 July 2026 the Viksit Bharat Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin) Act, 2025, known as VB-G RAM G, replaced MGNREGA, which stands repealed from the same date. The new law raises the guarantee from 100 days to 125 days of wage employment per rural household per financial year, and notified a daily wage floor of Rs 300. Cost sharing between the Centre and the States was reported as 60:40 for most States and 90:10 for eleven specified States. E-KYC verified MGNREGA job cards remain valid. Details are still being implemented, so users should check the Ministry of Rural Development.
