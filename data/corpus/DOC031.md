# Beti Bachao Beti Padhao

Beti Bachao Beti Padhao was launched on 22 January 2015 at Panipat in Haryana to address the declining Child Sex Ratio and related issues of women's empowerment. It is a tri-ministerial effort of the Ministry of Women and Child Development, the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare and the Ministry of Education. The objectives are to prevent gender-biased sex-selective elimination, to ensure the survival and protection of the girl child, and to ensure her education and participation. The campaign began in 100 districts with low Child Sex Ratio and was later expanded to cover the entire country. Its main activities include advocacy and awareness campaigns, community mobilisation, and stronger enforcement of the Pre-Conception and Pre-Natal Diagnostic Techniques Act of 1994, which prohibits sex determination. The Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana, a small savings account for the girl child, was introduced alongside the campaign. Progress is measured through indicators such as the Sex Ratio at Birth, institutional deliveries and the enrolment of girls in secondary education.
