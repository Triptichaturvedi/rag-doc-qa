# Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY)

Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana was launched on 8 April 2015 to provide loans to non-corporate, non-farm small and micro enterprises. MUDRA stands for Micro Units Development and Refinance Agency. Loans of up to Rs 10 lakh are given by banks, small finance banks, regional rural banks, microfinance institutions and non-banking finance companies for income-generating activities in manufacturing, trading and services, and the loans do not require collateral. Loans are classified into three categories according to the stage of growth of the business: Shishu covers loans up to Rs 50,000, Kishore covers loans above Rs 50,000 and up to Rs 5 lakh, and Tarun covers loans above Rs 5 lakh and up to Rs 10 lakh. A MUDRA debit card is issued to borrowers for withdrawing working capital. In the Union Budget 2024-25 the Government announced that the loan limit would be raised to Rs 20 lakh for entrepreneurs who had successfully repaid earlier Tarun loans, known as Tarun Plus. MUDRA itself refinances lenders and does not lend directly to individuals.
