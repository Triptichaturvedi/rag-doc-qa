# NIST SP 800-88 Guidelines for Media Sanitization

NIST Special Publication 800-88 provides guidance for sanitizing storage media so that data cannot be recovered when equipment is reused, donated or disposed of. It defines three sanitization methods. Clear applies logical techniques, such as overwriting storage locations with fixed data, to protect against simple non-invasive data recovery using standard interfaces. Purge applies physical or logical techniques that make recovery infeasible even with state-of-the-art laboratory methods, and examples include cryptographic erase, block erase on flash media and degaussing of magnetic media. Destroy renders the media unusable and data recovery infeasible, using methods such as shredding, disintegration, pulverising and incineration at a facility designed for this. The choice depends on the security categorisation of the information and on whether the media will leave the control of the organisation. Verification of the sanitization process is required, and a certificate of sanitization should record details such as the media type, the method used, the date and the person who performed it. Solid-state drives need special care because overwriting does not reliably reach all cells.
