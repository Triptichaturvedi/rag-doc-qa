# Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY)

Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY, launched on 23 September 2018, is a public health insurance scheme that provides a cover of Rs 5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary hospitalisation at empanelled public and private hospitals. It is implemented by the National Health Authority and is cashless and paperless: an eligible beneficiary shows an Ayushman card at the hospital. The scheme targets about 12 crore poor and vulnerable families identified using the Socio-Economic Caste Census 2011, and there is no cap on family size or age. Pre-existing conditions are covered from the first day, and expenses are covered for three days before admission and fifteen days after discharge. The cost of the scheme is shared between the Centre and the States. In 2024 the Government extended coverage to all citizens aged 70 years and above regardless of income, giving them a separate cover of Rs 5 lakh per year. Eligibility and package rates change, so patients should check with the National Health Authority. This summary is not medical advice.
