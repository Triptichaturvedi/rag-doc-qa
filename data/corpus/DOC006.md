# Rights against Exploitation, Freedom of Religion, Cultural and Educational Rights (Articles 23-30)

Article 23 prohibits traffic in human beings and forced labour, known as begar. Article 24 forbids the employment of children below the age of fourteen in factories, mines or other hazardous work. The Right to Freedom of Religion is contained in Articles 25 to 28. Article 25 guarantees freedom of conscience and the right to freely profess, practise and propagate religion, subject to public order, morality and health. Article 26 allows every religious denomination to manage its own religious affairs. Article 27 says no person can be compelled to pay taxes for the promotion of any particular religion, and Article 28 regulates religious instruction in educational institutions that are wholly maintained by the State. Cultural and Educational Rights are in Articles 29 and 30. Article 29 protects the interests of minorities by giving any section of citizens the right to conserve its distinct language, script or culture, while Article 30 gives minorities, based on religion or language, the right to establish and administer educational institutions of their choice.
