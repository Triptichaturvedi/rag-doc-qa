# Jal Jeevan Mission (Har Ghar Jal)

The Jal Jeevan Mission was announced on 15 August 2019 by the Ministry of Jal Shakti, with the goal of providing a Functional Household Tap Connection to every rural household, called Har Ghar Jal. The service standard is 55 litres of water per person per day of prescribed quality on a regular and long-term basis. At the time of launch, only about 17 percent of rural households had tap water connections. The mission is a Centrally Sponsored Scheme, and funding is shared between the Centre and the States in the ratio 90:10 for the Himalayan and North-Eastern States, 50:50 for other States, and 100 percent Central funding for Union Territories. The mission promotes community ownership: Village Water and Sanitation Committees or Pani Samitis plan, implement, operate and maintain the schemes, and a part of the cost is contributed by the community. Water quality is tested using field test kits. The original target date of 2024 was later extended, and a deadline of 2028 has been reported, so the current status should be read from the mission dashboard.
