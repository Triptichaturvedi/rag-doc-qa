# NIST SP 800-53 Security and Privacy Controls

NIST Special Publication 800-53 Revision 5 provides a catalog of security and privacy controls for information systems and organisations, and it is used by US federal agencies and widely by industry. The controls are grouped into 20 families, including Access Control, Awareness and Training, Audit and Accountability, Assessment, Authorization and Monitoring, Configuration Management, Contingency Planning, Identification and Authentication, Incident Response, Maintenance, Media Protection, Physical and Environmental Protection, Planning, Program Management, Personnel Security, Personally Identifiable Information Processing and Transparency, Risk Assessment, System and Services Acquisition, System and Communications Protection, System and Information Integrity, and Supply Chain Risk Management. Each control has a base statement, discussion and related controls. Control baselines of low, moderate and high impact are selected using FIPS 199 security categorisation of the system. Revision 5 made the controls outcome-based and independent of whether they are for federal use, and a separate publication, SP 800-53B, defines the baselines. Organisations tailor the baselines by adding or removing controls based on risk.
