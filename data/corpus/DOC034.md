# Right to Information Act, 2005 (RTI)

The Right to Information Act, 2005 lets any citizen request information from a public authority, and it came into full force on 12 October 2005. A citizen submits a written or online application to the Public Information Officer of the department, with a fee of Rs 10 for central government bodies, and the applicant does not have to give a reason for seeking the information. Persons below the poverty line are exempt from the fee. The Public Information Officer must provide the information within 30 days of the request, or within 48 hours if the information concerns the life or liberty of a person. If the applicant is unsatisfied or receives no reply, a first appeal can be made to a senior officer in the same public authority within 30 days, and a second appeal can be made to the Central Information Commission or the State Information Commission within 90 days. Section 4 requires public authorities to publish information proactively. Section 8 exempts some information from disclosure, such as matters affecting the sovereignty and security of the country, though information that cannot be denied to Parliament cannot be denied to a citizen.
