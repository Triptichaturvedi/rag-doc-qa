# The Supreme Court of India (Articles 124-147)

The Supreme Court of India was established under Article 124 and sits in New Delhi. It originally consisted of the Chief Justice of India and seven other judges, and the Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Amendment Act of 2019 raised the sanctioned strength to 34, including the Chief Justice. Judges are appointed by the President and retire at the age of 65. A judge can be removed only on the grounds of proved misbehaviour or incapacity through an address by both Houses of Parliament, supported by a majority of the total membership and two-thirds of the members present and voting. The Court has original jurisdiction in disputes between the Centre and the States under Article 131, appellate jurisdiction in constitutional, civil and criminal matters, and advisory jurisdiction under Article 143 when the President refers a question of law. Article 141 makes the law declared by the Supreme Court binding on all courts in India, and Article 142 allows it to pass orders necessary for doing complete justice. It is a court of record under Article 129 and has the power of judicial review.
